The Concept

Discovery, not another tasting room.

IBC is being created as an immersive environment for discovering finished beverage products across categories, styles and formats. The experience brings people closer to products while giving producers and industry partners a new way to connect with consumers.

The Discovery Laboratory

The laboratory is the environment.

IBC isn't a traditional consulting laboratory and isn't about telling a producer how to formulate its product. The products have already been developed.

Our focus is creating an environment where people can experience finished beverages in a highly interactive setting.

Beverage categories can extend far beyond beer: wine, cider, hard seltzer, non-alcoholic beverages, coffee and caffeinated products, experimental cocktails and other emerging formats.

Technology can make the experience more personal, measurable and engaging while creating new opportunities for participating brands.
