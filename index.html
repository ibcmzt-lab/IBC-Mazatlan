The Concept

The Laboratory

The laboratory is the experience.

IBC isn't about reformulating a producer's product or telling a brand what its beverage should taste like.

The products have already been developed. Our role is to create an environment where people can experience them.

Technology can make sampling more interactive, personal and measurable while creating new ways for consumers and participating brands to connect.

The result is a discovery environment built around curiosity, participation and finished products.
