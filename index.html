The Concept

A laboratory for
beverage discovery.

IBC creates an immersive setting where people can explore finished beverages in a way that is social, interactive and technology-forward.

This is not a product-development lab. The products already exist. The experience is about discovering them.

From emerging Mexican producers to established beverage brands, IBC creates a space where consumers can sample, compare and discover products across categories.

Beer, wine, cider, hard seltzer, non-alcoholic beverages, coffee and caffeinated drinks, experimental cocktails, and whatever comes next can all have a place at the wall.

Technology adds another dimension: interaction, participation, personal discovery and a growing picture of what people actually choose to pour.
