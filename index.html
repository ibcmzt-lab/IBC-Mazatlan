The Concept

A laboratory for beverage discovery.

IBC is being developed as an immersive tasting environment where people can explore a wide range of beverages in a completely different way.

The experience brings together self-pour technology, social interaction and direct consumer feedback in one environment.

The goal is simple: make discovering something new more engaging, more personal and more useful for both consumers and beverage producers.
What's Pouring

More than beer.

IBC is designed to explore the full spectrum of beverage possibilities, with a special focus on Mexico-forward producers and emerging categories.

Beer

Lagers, ales, IPAs and distinctive Mexican craft selections.

Wine

Mexican wines, regional discoveries and creative wine-based pours.

Cider

An emerging category with plenty of room for discovery.

Hard Seltzer

Modern, refreshing and experimental flavor profiles.

Non-Alcoholic

NA beverages that deserve a place at the tasting table.

Coffee

Coffee and caffeinated discoveries beyond the traditional café.

Experimental

New ideas, unusual combinations and products still finding their audience.

Cocktails

Creative cocktails and beverage concepts built for discovery.

The Experience

Taste it. Rate it. Discover what comes next.

IBC is being designed to make consumer feedback part of the experience, creating a living picture of what people actually want to drink.

  • Explore multiple beverages in one visit
  • Pour only what you want to taste
  • Record your reactions and preferences
  • See what other people are discovering
  • Help producers understand real consumer demand
