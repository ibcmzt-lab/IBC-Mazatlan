The Concept

A New Way to Experience Craft Beverages

Created by Immersive Beverage Concepts (IBC), Uno Más bridges the gap between craft culture and self-guided tasting technology. Explore small-batch brews at your own pace without waiting for a server.

Total Pour Freedom

Taste an ounce or pour a full glass. Discover your personal preferences through sample-sized exploration across diverse beverage styles.

Seamless Cashless Tech

Check in, receive your RFID tasting pass, and unlock draft lines effortlessly. Your tab tracks precisely what you pour down to the milliliter.

Interactive Tasting Wall

Digital screens displaying ABV, flavor profiles, brewer notes, and real-time tap levels right above every single faucet.

Self-Serve Lineup

The Draft Tap Matrix

A curated selection calibrated for proper head presentation and serving temperatures across multiple liquid categories.

Craft Lager & Ale

From clean Bavarian-style helles and Kellerbiers to bold IPAs and Belgian monk ales. Feature selections alongside local Mexican microbreweries.

  • Classic European Benchmark Lagers
  • Trappist Ales & Rauchbiers
  • Local Small-Batch IPAs

Seltzers & Ferments

Crisp hard seltzers infused with local fruit profiles, fermented kombuchas, botanical elixirs, and sparkling ciders.

  • Natural Fruit Botanical Seltzers
  • Fermented Tepache & Kombuchas
  • Low-ABV Refreshers

Nitro & Cold Drafts

Creamy nitrogen-infused dark brews, experimental stouts, artisanal cold brew coffee, and sparkling infused teas.

  • Velvet Nitro Pour Stouts
  • Cold Brew Draft Taps
  • Barrel-Aged Single Batches
For Independent Producers

A Testing Ground for Beverage Creators

Uno Más operates as a brand incubator for craft breweries, spirit makers, and beverage innovators. Test new recipes, measure pour data in real time, and gather direct guest feedback before commercial scaling.

Pour-Data & Consumer Feedback

Track pour velocity, volume per session, and real-time consumer ratings.

Limited-Run Test Batches

Feature small-batch recipes on rotating taps for immediate market validation.
